TAPE

A working preview of the Android app, to review before building it.

  • Audio really plays. The sample tracks are generated in your browser. To hear your own, use Add files in Music, or Choose folder in Books.
  • Drime is simulated. Paste any token of 8+ characters to see sample folders. Nothing leaves your browser.
  • Things to try: resume a book after 2 minutes (it steps back 10 s), change a book's speed, set a 1-minute sleep timer, and add a Drime folder to Books.
  • Progress and settings are saved in this browser only.
